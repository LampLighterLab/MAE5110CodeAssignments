### Written report

A PDF "extended abstract" of your project, following the formatting guidelines from [Assignment 5](assignment_5.md) and [Assignment 6](assignment_6.md). You are allowed up to 3 pages for content, plus additional pages for the bibliography.

A standard manuscript follows the following format:

- **Introduction.** Briefly set the context and motivation for your project (what is the gap?) and state your contribution.
- **Related Work.** Remember to focus on _fencing in_. You only need to point out related work that helps contextualize your contribution. Make the contrast clear!
- **Methods.** Explain your implementation and methodology. A block diagram can be very helpful here.
- **Experiments.** Explain how you are evaluating your implementation.
- **Results & Discussion.** Show your actual results and discuss how they should be interpreted.
- **Conclusion & Outlook.** Briefly summarize what the reader should take away and what you would do next.

This is a lot to fit into 3 pages, so focus on what matters most. The introduction and related work should be especially concise. You may combine Introduction and Related Work, and also Methods and Experiments, as long as each part is clear (for example, by using separate paragraphs).

Follow the advice in [Whitesides’ Group: Writing a Paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.200400767); use Assignment 6 as your draft and revise it as you run experiments and get results. Early results will often help you refine your contribution and adjust the next set of experiments to sharpen your claims.

**Important:** your claims should be validated in a quantified way!

### Video

Create a 2–3 minute video abstract of your work.
The video has the same intent and should follow the same overall structure as the written report, but highly compressed. You may omit related work here, but do make the motivation and gap clear. Be selective about which experiments and details you explain, and focus more on the results, especially hardware results. You can also show "cool" simulations if some things didn't transfer properly; if sim to real transfer didn't work at all, do show a clip of the failed attempt and follow up with commentary of why you believe it failed. Showing sim and hardware side by side is ideal.

We recommend making a screen recording of a presentation. Zoom works well for this. If you prefer to skip recording your own voice-over, you may use AI narration with captions.
