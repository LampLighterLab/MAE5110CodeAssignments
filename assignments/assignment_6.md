# Assignment 6

**Submission Deadline:** Monday, November 9th, 11:59PM (midnight)

**Grading:**

- 20 points for completion.
- 10 points for quality of assessments.

**Goals:**

- Practice outcome-driven project planning
  - Translate intuitive desired outcomes into concrete, testable experiments
  - Practice clear and concise writing
- Prepare for Sim-to-real transfer

## Deliverable

A PDF outline of your proposed project, following the general guidelines from [Assignment 5](assignment_5.md). You are allowed up to 2 pages this time, but aim for the same length as before; shorter is better. The extra page should really only be needed if you are including figures, e.g. block diagrams or graphs.
Make clear what your quantitative assessment criteria will be, and make a distinction between criteria applied in simulation and/or hardware, and criteria for evaluating whether you are ready for transition to hardware (sim-to-real).
Your assessments should directly test your project’s intended outcome.
General criteria such as success rate can be useful, but explain the task and conditions under which you will measure them, and what the results would let you conclude.
As a rule of thumb, for each criterion, imagine some of the other projects you've seen and if the criterion would make sense there. If yes, it may be too general, and try to make it more precise and specific to your outcome.
Some assessments are very general and fine to have, but you should have at least one core assessment that is specific to your project.

In addition, open a _Draft PR_ from your branch to the upstream [BigRedGym](https://github.com/LampLighterLab/BigRedGym).
Then make a post on [EdStem](https://edstem.org/us/courses/103446/discussion), with a screen recording of your work in progress, aiming for sim-to-real deployment. Include your project report (you should be able to copy/paste it, Ed supports most markdown) and include a link to your PR.
Remember, EdStem is a place for conversation, and you can add/change the content as you go. Just like with a _Draft PR_, it's better to start the post early, and then work towards completion, than to post it at the deadline.

Once you think your policy is ready for sim-to-real transfer (or if you're wondering whether or not it is), notify the course staff so we can take a look and provide feedback, and start scheduling some time for testing.
By the deadline, your criteria for sim-to-real don't need to pass, but they need to be evaluated (and those evaluations posted in your Ed project post).
